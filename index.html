How this works
  • Every day the price of each house is checked for every check-in date in the next 45 days, for 2 adults, and kept. Price per night is the Airbnb total with all fees, before taxes, divided by the nights quoted (usually the minimum stay). A night no stay can start on, because of a minimum stay or a booking right after it, takes the per-night price of the stay that covers it.
  • When a night that was open gets booked, the last price seen for it is kept and used as its likely booking price. These fill in as the daily checks pile up.
  • Calls: Reduce when you are more than 10% above peers while they still have room, or above them with a week or less to go. Increase when peers are mostly booked and you are at or below their price. Otherwise Keep. Suggested prices are rounded to $5.
  • Peers leave out "1221AB (2nd listing)", the same house as "1221 Ab", and "Themed villa (Julie)", whose calendar is closed.
